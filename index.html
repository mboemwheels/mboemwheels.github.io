MB OEM Wheels

MB OEM Wheels is an independent reference for factory Mercedes-Benz wheels by part number: about 1,158 designs across 85 chassis and 2,274 part numbers.

How to identify a factory Mercedes wheel

Look on the back of the wheel or inside a spoke for a part number that starts with A followed by ten digits, for example A2054010700. The first three digits after the A tell you the chassis: 205 is the C-Class W205, 213 is the E-Class W213, 253 is the GLC X253 and 167 is the GLE W167.

Search that number on mboemwheels.com to see the design name, size, offset and which models it came on.
